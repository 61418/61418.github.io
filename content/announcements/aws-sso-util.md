[aws-sso-util](https://github.com/61418/aws-sso-util), written by [Ben Kehoe](https://github.com/benkehoe), has been a popular tool for years. We are happy to share that Ben has decided to transfer ownership of aws-sso-util to 61418. In the coming weeks, we will release a project roadmap for the project and begin tackling user issues in the coming months.
